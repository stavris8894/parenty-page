Medicine safety

Know what's due before you give it.

For every child, see each medicine with when the last dose was given, at home or by you, and when the next one is allowed.

  • The last dose time, even one given at home this morning
  • A warning before a dose that's too early
  • Log a dose in one tap, and parents see it straight away

Questions from daycares

How do I start Daycare mode?

Create a free Parenty account, then tap the title at the top of the screen and choose Daycare mode, or go to Settings → App mode. Set up your daycare's name and username, and you're ready for your first family.

How do parents invite me?

Parents with Premium go to Settings → Account → Daycare access and type your daycare username, or scan the QR code you show them from Daycare mode. You accept the invitation, and the family appears in your list.

What can I see of a family?

Only the children the parents chose and their medicines, with when the last dose was given and when the next one is allowed. You don't see the family's own records, doctor visits, reports or notes, and you can't change their medicines or children.

What happens when access ends?

Access ends on the date the parents chose, or when they end it, and the family leaves your list. If the family's Premium ends, access pauses until it's back. Everything you logged stays in your History.

Can a nanny or grandparent use Daycare mode?

Yes. Daycare mode is for anyone who looks after other families' children. It's free for one family invited by a Premium parent, so a nanny or grandparent helping one family doesn't pay.

Does Daycare mode work offline?

No. It needs an internet connection, so parents see every entry straight away and you never give a dose based on an old last-dose time. Nothing about a family is kept on your phone.

Can I still use Parenty for my own family?

Yes. One account can be both. Switch between Daycare mode and your own family from the title at the top of the screen. Parenty Daycare includes Premium for your own family.
