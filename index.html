About the App

Parenty is the essential companion for parents, designed to take the stress out of managing your children's health. Whether you’re dealing with a common cold or managing long-term treatments, Parenty helps you stay organized, ensures doses are never missed, and keeps a clear record of your child's recovery journey.

Built by parents, for parents, our app combines a powerful medicine scheduler with an intuitive symptom tracker, making it easy to coordinate care across multiple children.

When your child is unwell, the last thing you want to worry about is "When did I give the last dose?" or "Is it time for more medicine?". Parenty provides a clear, reliable source of truth for the whole family, ensuring everyone is on the same page.
