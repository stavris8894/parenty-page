Privacy Policy

Parenty: Medicine Tracker

1. Introduction

Parenty: Medicine Tracker (“Parenty”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) is developed by Stavros Georgiou. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information.

Parenty is designed with privacy in mind. All personal and health-related data is stored securely on your device and is never shared with us unless you explicitly choose to contact support.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter in the App

Parenty allows you to store information related to your children's health, such as:

  • Child’s name or nickname
  • Medicine schedules and dosage information
  • Symptom logs
  • Health-related notes

All of this information is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store it.

2.2 Contact Information (Support Emails)

If you contact us at stavris.8894@gmail.com, we may receive:

  • Your email address
  • Details you include in your message

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry.

2.3 App Usage Data

Parenty does not collect analytics, tracking data, advertising data, device identifiers, or location information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use information you provide directly (e.g., via email) for:

  • Responding to support requests
  • Improving the app based on user feedback

On-device data is used only to provide app functionality and never leaves your device.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 On-Device Data

  • All health and personal data is stored locally on your iOS device.
  • You maintain full control over this information.
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data.

4.2 Support Communications

Emails are securely stored by our email provider and are never shared with third parties.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, or rent any personal or health data. Information may be shared only if required by law.

6. Children’s Privacy

Parenty is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not by children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights such as:

  • Accessing information you provided
  • Requesting deletion of support communication
  • Withdrawing consent for communication

Because app data is stored only on your device, you can edit or delete it at any time through device settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Parenty does not use any external services, analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party APIs.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes will be posted on this page.
