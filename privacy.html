Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Parenty, operated by Stavros Georgiou ("we", "us", "our"), protecting your family's personal health information is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the Parenty application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide medication scheduling and health tracking, Parenty collects:

  • Profile Information: Child names or display pseudonyms, profile avatars, and optional weight logs.
  • Medication & Health Data: Medicine names, dosage schedules, temperature readings, and fever log entries.
  • Account Credentials: Email address for account authentication and multi-device synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide and improve the app's functionality:

  • Sending timely dose reminder alerts and notifications.
  • Syncing health logs across your family’s authorized devices.
  • Generating exportable health reports for your family and pediatrician.

3. Data Protection & Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit and AES encryption at rest) to ensure your data remains secure and private.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal or health data to advertisers or third parties under any circumstances.
