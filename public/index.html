Help & Support

Have questions or need assistance? We're here to help.

How does device synchronization work?

Parenty automatically syncs medicine logs across all signed-in devices in real time so everyone stays updated.

Can I manage multiple children?

Yes! You can create individual profiles for each child with dedicated schedules, dosages, and temperature charts.

Troubleshooting Checklist
